How to Use Medident Sensitive Care

1

Apply Proper Amount

Squeeze a pea-sized amount of Medident Sensitive Care onto a soft-bristled toothbrush.

2

Brush Gently

Brush your teeth gently for 2 minutes, paying special attention to sensitive areas.

3

Rinse Thoroughly

Rinse your mouth thoroughly with water after brushing.

4

Use Twice Daily

For best results, use twice daily - morning and before bedtime.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use consistently for at least 2 weeks to see full benefits
  • Avoid eating or drinking for 30 minutes after brushing
  • Replace your toothbrush every 3 months
  • Consult your dentist for persistent sensitivity issues

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I see results?

Most users experience relief from sensitivity within the first week of regular use. For optimal results, continue using for at least 2 weeks.

Is Medident safe for daily use?

Yes, Medident Sensitive Care is formulated for daily use and has been clinically tested for safety. It's gentle enough to use twice daily.

Can children use this toothpaste?

Medident Sensitive Care is recommended for ages 12 and above. For younger children, consult with a pediatric dentist.

Does this toothpaste contain fluoride?

Yes, Medident contains 1450ppm sodium fluoride, which is the optimal amount recommended by dental professionals for cavity prevention.
